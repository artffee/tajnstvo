01 / MARCH · МАРТ

Martenitsa

A wish carried by a thread.

On 1 March, red and white threads are made, given and worn to welcome spring. A blossoming tree or the sight of a stork or swallow can mark the moment to remove them. Related customs are shared across Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Moldova and Romania.

02 / JANUARY · ЯНУАРИ

Masks & new beginnings

A village becomes a stage.

Bulgaria’s masquerade traditions vary by region. In the Pernik region, Surova is celebrated on 13–14 January. Survakari wear handmade masks and costumes, gather in the village and visit homes. The living custom is passed between generations; it is distinct from the city’s staged festival programme.

03 / JUNE · ЮНИ

Nestinarstvo

Memory held in movement.

In the village of Bulgari in Strandzha, Nestinarstvo forms part of the annual Panagyr for Saints Constantine and Helena on 3–4 June. Its fire-dancing rite belongs to a living community tradition, with music, shared memory and religious meaning.
