Privacy

Updated 26 October 2026

Your birth profile

Your name, birth date, time and selected birthplace are calculated in your browser and stored in sessionStorage for this tab, including through checkout and refresh. Closing the tab normally clears them; browser session recovery may retain them. They are not sent to the payment server.

City search

Common cities are matched locally. Other search text is sent to Photon (Komoot / OpenStreetMap) to find coordinates. Like any network service, that provider can receive your IP address. Birth date, name and time are not included in the city-search request. Time-zone lookup then runs locally.

Optional AI

The standard reading needs no API key. If you explicitly activate optional AI in Explore, your chart summary and any ritual text you submit can be sent directly to Anthropic. Their terms, retention rules and usage charges apply. Your key is stored only in this tab’s session storage; the page code can access it while active. Never enter a shared or unrestricted organizational key.

Payments

When enabled, Stripe handles payment and billing details. Our server sends the selected product, price, report language and a hashed random browser identifier, not your birth profile. Stripe retains transaction records. Essential HttpOnly cookies connect a verified purchase with this browser for repeat downloads. Refunds revoke access on the next verification.

Local tools and preferences

Your language preference and the 45-day companion’s chart snapshot and progress use localStorage until cleared. Camera and microphone samples in the experimental Frequency Reader are processed locally. No analytics or advertising tags are loaded by this version. Hosting providers still process ordinary request logs. Some original Explore pages load fonts from Google.

Private support

The private support address is being configured. Purchases remain unavailable until support and checkout are ready.

Share cards and daily progress

Share cards are generated in your browser. Birth date and location are excluded; adding your name is optional. Nothing is posted automatically. The daily ritual stores up to 31 completion entries on this device until cleared. It does not send your birth profile to a server.

Relationship readings and journal

The second birth chart is held in page memory and is not saved. Journal entries are stored in this browser’s localStorage without encryption, until you delete them or clear local data. Anyone using this browser profile can read them. Journal backups contain your text. Import reads a local backup in the browser; it does not upload it to a server.

Purchase recovery

Essential purchase cookies remember up to three recent checkout sessions for one year, including pending purchases, so this browser can recover after an interrupted return from Stripe. Clearing local readings does not remove these HttpOnly cookies. Clearing browser cookies or changing device requires private support with your receipt; there is no account-based recovery. Downloaded PDF copies cannot be remotely revoked.
