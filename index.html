    BEFORE YOU BEGIN

    A few good questions.

    What is free?

    Your personal on-screen preview and the tools in Explore are free. PDF downloads are separate one-time purchases and stay unavailable until checkout is ready.

    Do I need my exact birth time?

    A known local birth time is needed for Rising, houses, astrocartography and the Star Map. Choose “I don’t know my birth time” for a limited noon-based preview; even the Moon sign can change during a day.

    Do I need an AI subscription or API key?

    No. Your free reading and the written PDF editions use authored interpretations matched to your chart. Optional AI narration is an advanced feature in Explore, with separate consent and provider costs.

    Are my birth details private?

    Calculations run in your browser. Your profile is kept in this tab’s session storage so it can survive checkout and refresh. City searches may go to Photon. Optional AI sends chart details to Anthropic only when you activate it. Read Privacy for the full explanation.

    Which languages are available?

    The homepage, free preview, new personal tools and written reports support English and Bulgarian. Choose the report language before purchase. The original Explore tools and Star Map are in English.

    How precise are the calculations?

    The original simplified astronomical engine is preserved. Time zones use geographic estimates and the browser’s historical time-zone data. Locations near time-zone boundaries and older records deserve extra checking. Astrology is a symbolic practice, not a validated way to predict events.
